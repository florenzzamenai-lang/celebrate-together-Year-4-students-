02 • Real-life connection

Celebrations Around Us

People in Malaysia celebrate different special days with family, food, clothes, decorations, and fun activities.

🌙

Hari Raya Aidilfitri

Families visit one another and enjoy special food.

🪔

Deepavali

Families may decorate their homes with lights and colourful designs.

🏮

Chinese New Year

Lanterns and red decorations can be seen.

🎄

Christmas

Some families decorate a tree and exchange gifts.

🌾

Gawai & Kaamatan

Communities celebrate culture, harvest, and togetherness.

🎂

Birthday

People may share a cake, gifts, and wishes.

❤️
Remember: Not every family celebrates the same way. Listen respectfully and learn from one another.
